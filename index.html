07 — THE PERSON

BEHIND THE RHYTHM

Rhythm has always been more than music for Akbar — it is a language, a feeling, a conversation between performer and audience. From an early age, Akbar was drawn to the pulse of percussion, the raw energy of live performance and the ability of a single beat to transform an atmosphere.

Performing on Octapad, Dhol and various percussion instruments, Akbar brings a unique combination of technical skill, spontaneous energy and genuine passion to every stage. His style blends the richness of traditional South Asian music with the precision and dynamism of modern live performance.

Whether performing at an intimate private gathering or commanding a large wedding stage, Akbar's focus is always the same: to create a moment that the audience will remember long after the music stops.
